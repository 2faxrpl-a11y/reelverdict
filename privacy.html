Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains what Reel Verdict Poster ("the tool") and The Reel Verdict website collect and why.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by The Reel Verdict to post to its own accounts. It does not let other people sign in.

What we collect

  • When we authorise the tool, TikTok and Instagram give it an access token and basic profile information for our own account (for example, display name and account ID).
  • After posting, we read performance numbers for our own videos (views, likes, comments, shares) to decide what to post next.

What we don't do

We do not collect data about other users, sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties. The website has no tracking, cookies or analytics.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer with restricted file permissions and are never published. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok or Instagram app settings.

Deletion

Removing the tool's access in TikTok or Instagram settings stops all access; we delete stored tokens on request or when access is revoked.

Contact

Send a direct message to @reelverdict.tv on Instagram (instagram.com/reelverdict.tv) or TikTok.
