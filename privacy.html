Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains what Reel Verdict Poster ("the tool") and The Reel Verdict website collect and why.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used by The Reel Verdict's team. Each team member signs in with their own TikTok account and can only post to that account.

What we collect

  • When we authorise the tool, TikTok and Instagram give it an access token and basic profile information for the signed-in account (display name, avatar and account ID), used only to show which account a video will be posted to.
  • After posting, we read performance numbers for our own videos (views, likes, comments, shares) to decide what to post next.

What we don't do

We do not collect data about other users, sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties. The website has no tracking, cookies or analytics.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the signed-in team member's own computer with restricted file permissions and are never published. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok or Instagram app settings.

Deletion

Removing the tool's access in TikTok or Instagram settings stops all access; we delete stored tokens on request or when access is revoked.

Contact

Send a direct message to @reelverdict.tv on Instagram (instagram.com/reelverdict.tv) or TikTok.
