Reel Verdict Poster

Reel Verdict Poster is our team's publishing tool. Each team member signs in with their own TikTok account, picks a video they made, chooses who can see it and whether others can comment, duet or stitch, and posts it to their own account. It never posts to an account without that person signing in and pressing Post.
